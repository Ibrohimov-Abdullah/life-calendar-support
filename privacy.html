Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Life Calendar ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our application and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

Life Calendar is designed with privacy at its core. We collect minimal information necessary to provide you with the best experience:

  • Account information (if you sign in with Google or Apple).
  • Goals, notes, and preferences you enter within the app.
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to improve app stability.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve Life Calendar features, sync your progress across devices, and respond to support inquiries.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@lifecalendar.app.
