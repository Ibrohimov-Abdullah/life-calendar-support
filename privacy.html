Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Life Calendar does not collect, store on our servers, or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the app uses

To draw your life as a grid of boxes and count the days left, the app asks for information such as your birthdate and the age you expect to reach. This information stays on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you can remove all of your data at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone.

Children's privacy

Life Calendar does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
