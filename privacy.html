Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Life Calendar ("we," "our," or "us") is a free iPhone app that shows your life as a grid of boxes and tells you how many days are left until the end of the year. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: we do not collect your personal data.

2. Information we collect

Life Calendar does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us. There is no account or sign-in, and the app has no ads, purchases, or third-party tracking.

Any information you enter into the app, such as your birthdate and preferences, is used only to draw your calendar and stays on your device.

3. How your information is used and stored

Your information is stored locally on your iPhone and used only to provide the app's features. We cannot see it, and we do not have access to it.

4. Sharing your information

Because we do not collect your data, we do not sell, rent, or share it with anyone. Apple may provide us with anonymous crash or usage statistics if you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings. This information cannot be used to identify you.

5. Deleting your data

All of your data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone.

6. Children's privacy

Life Calendar does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at abdullahibrokhimovofficial@gmail.com. We only use your email address to reply to you.
