Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Life Calendar ("we," "our," or "us") is an iPhone app that helps you visualize your life in weeks and track personal goals. This policy explains what information the app handles, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Life Calendar you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic details that provider shares with us, such as your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address).
  • Content you enter. Your birthdate, goals, notes, and app preferences.
  • Usage and diagnostic data. Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports that help us fix bugs and improve stability. This data is not used to identify you personally.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features.
  • To sync your progress across your devices, if you choose to sign in.
  • To find and fix bugs and improve performance.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Where your data is stored

Your goals, notes, and preferences are stored on your device. If you sign in and enable sync, a copy is also stored with our cloud service provider so it can be restored on your other devices.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with service providers that help us run the app (sign-in, cloud storage, analytics, and crash reporting), and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's settings. If you cannot access the app, email us at support@lifecalendar.app from the address linked to your account and we will delete your data. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

7. Your choices and rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your information at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection laws. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

Life Calendar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also notify you in the app.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@lifecalendar.app.
