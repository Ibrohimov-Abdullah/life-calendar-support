Reporting a bug? Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version (Settings → About), and the steps that led to the problem.

Frequently asked questions

How is my data backed up?

Your data is stored on your device. If you sign in and turn on sync, it is also backed up to your cloud account so you can restore it on another device.

How do I restore my data on a new iPhone?

Install Life Calendar on the new phone and sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before. Your goals and notes will sync back automatically.

Can I change my birthdate or other settings?

Yes. Open the app settings at any time to change your birthdate and preferences. Your weeks calendar updates right away.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Delete account. This removes your account and all synced data. If you can't open the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

What data does Life Calendar collect?

Only what the app needs to work: your sign-in details, the goals and notes you enter, and anonymous usage and crash data. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
