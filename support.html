Frequently asked questions

What does Life Calendar do?

It shows your life as a grid of boxes so you can see how much time has passed and how much is ahead. It also shows how many days are left until the end of the year.

Is the app really free?

Yes. Life Calendar is completely free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

Can I change my birthdate or other settings?

Yes. Open the app settings at any time to change your birthdate and preferences. Your calendar updates right away.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. We don't collect your data and there is no account. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Because all data stays on your device, deleting the app removes it.
